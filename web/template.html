Arc Aftersales Agent

AI-first service desk · prototype

An AI agent that answers riders and dealers, reads each bike's telemetry to act before the rider notices, and settles warranty claims through fixed rules. Safety issues and doubtful cases go straight to a person.

Built by Luis Rayón as part of his application to Stark Future. Arc Moto, its bikes, customers and warranty policy are fictional; the policy is modelled on EU and Spanish consumer law.

ListensChat with a rider or dealer, in their language.
Looks upBike record, live telemetry alerts, knowledge base, parts stock.
Decides by rulesA deterministic engine decides coverage. The model never does.
Hands overSafety, legal doubt or missing evidence go to a person with a ready summary.
